-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HM (HEVC Test Model) reference software, version 16.20
https://vcgit.hhi.fraunhofer.de/jvet/HM
Ported parts: the HEVC intra decoder in src/PictureSharp.Heif/Hevc (CABAC engine and context tables, syntax parsing,
QP derivation, scaling lists, dequantization, inverse transforms, intra prediction, deblocking filter, sample adaptive
offset) and the intra encoder in src/PictureSharp.Heif/Hevc/Encoder (CABAC encoder and rate estimation, syntax writing,
forward transforms, quantization, rate-distortion optimized quantization and sign data hiding, sample adaptive offset
estimation, intra mode and coding unit decisions). Each ported method carries a
"port: HM ..." source comment.
